# 🧴 Glue Flip

**Glue Flip** is an ultra-realistic 3D physics game where your goal is simple: **throw a glue stick and land it perfectly upright.**

## 🎮 About

A small physics-based game focused on realistic:

* 🧴 Glue stick design and materials
* ⚙️ Rotation and momentum
* 🪂 Gravity and trajectory
* 🫨 Bounces and collisions
* 🪵 Surface friction and impact
* 🎥 Immersive 3D camera

Every throw is different. Control your launch and try to build the longest possible combo.

## 🏆 Features

* Realistic 3D physics
* Multiple environments and surfaces
* Progressive challenges
* Score & combo system
* Best score tracking
* Immersive visual design
* Responsive controls

## 🚀 Goal

**Throw. Rotate. Land. Repeat.**

Can you master the perfect landing?
