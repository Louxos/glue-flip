# 🕊️ Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want **Glue Flip** to be a welcoming place for anyone who loves physics, games or
code — regardless of experience, background, identity or how many glue sticks they
have failed to land upright.

## Our Standards

**Be kind.** Be curious. Assume good intent.

Helpful behaviour includes:

-  Welcoming newcomers and constructive, respectful feedback.
- 🌍 Owning mistakes and learning from them.
-  Focusing on what is best for the project.

Unacceptable behaviour includes:

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Trolling, derogatory comments, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, in issues and pull requests, and in any space where
someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behaviour can be reported by opening an issue or contacting the
maintainers. Reports are taken seriously and handled confidentially.
